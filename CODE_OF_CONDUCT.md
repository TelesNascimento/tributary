# Code of conduct

We want this to be a friendly, professional place to work on a developer tool.

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good faith and give constructive feedback.
- No harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Keep discussions on topic and free of confidential or private information.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions that do not follow these rules. To report a problem,
contact the maintainer through the address on the GitHub profile.
